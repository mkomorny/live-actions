# Professional Mixing & Mastering: Urban, Bass & Trap Genres

Comprehensive mixing and mastering blueprints for Drill, Trap, UK Garage, and Phonk.

## Drill Engineering Blueprint

**Target Metrics:** Style: Drill
Current LUFS: -18
True Peak: -3.2

### Production Reasoning (<think>)
<think>
Mixing Philosophy for Drill:
- Clarity in low-mids is king
- Controlled dynamics with punch
- Modern loudness without destroying transients

**Mix Bus Chain (Ableton Live 12):**
1. Group all tracks → "Mix Bus"
2. EQ Eight: High-pass 25Hz (-24dB), surgical cuts at 250Hz (-3dB, Q 2.8), air boost +2.5dB @ 12kHz
3. Glue Compressor: Threshold -18dB, Ratio 2.5:1, Attack 15ms, Release 80ms
4. Multiband Dynamics: Gentle compression on 200-800Hz band

**Mastering Chain:**
1. EQ Eight (linear phase): Subtle low-end shelf +2dB @ 60Hz, air +1.8dB @ 14kHz
2. Compressor: Threshold -14dB, Ratio 1.8:1, Attack 8ms
3. Limiter (stock or iZotope): Ceiling -0.3dB, True Peak limiting
4. Target: -8 to -9 LUFS integrated, -1.0dB True Peak

Reasoning: Drill needs energy and clarity. Over-compression kills groove. We use parallel processing and surgical EQ instead of heavy limiting.
</think>

**Exact Actions in Ableton:**
1. Create "Mix Bus" group
2. Add plugins in order above with exact settings
3. Use "Utility" for mono check below 120Hz
4. Export with "Normalize" off, Dither on

This chain delivers competitive loudness while preserving the life of the beat.
