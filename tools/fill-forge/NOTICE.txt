Fill Forge
==========

Fill Forge is adapted from Breakdowns, an MIT-licensed project by another author:

  https://github.com/dnvsfn/ableton-breakdowns
  Copyright (c) 2026 E. T. Carter
  License: MIT (see LICENSE in this folder)

The original musical engine (genre banks, techniques, humanize/swing) and the
dialog are kept. The name was changed for this app; the work is still theirs.

Changes for Live Actions:
  - Hosted as a desktop tool (Max for Live bridge), not a Suite Extension
  - Insert destinations: New clip, Replace, Append to end, New track, At playhead
  - Insert posts pitched MIDI notes to the Live Actions host
